The science behind our adventure

The facts on this website are educational approximations. Some values, especially ocean depth, vary by survey and measurement method.

Trusted places to verify ocean facts

  • NOAA National Ocean Service — Ocean facts (online reference only)
  • NOAA Ocean Exploration — Deep ocean and ocean exploration
  • NASA Earth Observatory — Earth, oceans, and climate
  • UNESCO Ocean Literacy — Ocean literacy resources

Images and code

All included illustrations (Earth, coral reef, and deep-sea anglerfish) are original SVG graphics created for this project, not downloaded photographs. Emoji appearances vary by computer. Website structure and interactive JavaScript were generated with AI assistance and should be reviewed and personalized by the student.

External reference links are optional and require internet. The website itself works without internet.

Accuracy notes

Ocean surface coverage is approximately 71%. Everest's elevation is approximately 8,849 metres. Challenger Deep is approximately 10,900–11,000 metres deep depending on the survey. Ocean-zone boundaries are approximate. The map is schematic, not a navigation chart.
